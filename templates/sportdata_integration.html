What does this do?

Once an Event ID is set, a 🔄 Sync live times button appears on the fight cards and schedule pages. It fetches the current timetable from sportdata.org and compares it with the times parsed from your PDF.

Each fight shows a ● +12m (running late) or ● −5m (running ahead) badge next to its scheduled time. The page auto-refreshes every 60 seconds while open.
